My research asks how learning systems can inherit the discipline of control: guarantees, structure, robustness, and an explicit account of dynamics.

<div class="theme-grid">
  <section class="research-theme">
    <h3>Learning-based control with guarantees</h3>
    <p>I study controller and observer designs that learn from data while preserving safety, stability, and robustness. Current directions include Neural ODE controllers, differentiable optimization layers, Control Lyapunov Functions, and Control Barrier Functions.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-theme">
    <h3>Control-informed machine learning</h3>
    <p>Control theory offers useful ways to think about training dynamics, model depth, time horizons, and optimality. I use these perspectives to accelerate continuous-depth learning and make model behavior easier to reason about.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-theme">
    <h3>Safe and reliable generative models</h3>
    <p>Generative models are increasingly used inside technical workflows. I am interested in reliability constraints, safe generation, and ways to connect learned generative behavior with formal control objectives.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-theme">
    <h3>Neural ODEs and continuous-time learning</h3>
    <p>Neural ODEs provide a natural bridge between learning and dynamical systems. My work explores their use for robust observers, efficient inference, and safe controller synthesis.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## Selected Directions

- Neural ODE observers for nonlinear systems with partially known or unknown dynamics.
- Minimum-time and Lyapunov-inspired training mechanisms for continuous-depth models.
- Differentiable optimization layers for safe Neural ODE controller design.
- Reinforcement learning algorithms with state-wise stability and safety guarantees.
