abstract: >-
  This paper studies an optimal control model for growth and consumption, formulated through continuous-time dynamic optimization. The analysis characterizes optimal policies for the evolution of capital and consumption, with attention to path constraints and the qualitative structure of optimal trajectories. It connects classical economic growth modeling with rigorous optimal-control methods, using necessary conditions and boundary-arc reasoning to understand how optimal consumption decisions interact with long-run growth dynamics.
