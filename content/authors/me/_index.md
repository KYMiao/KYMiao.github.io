I am a DPhil candidate in the [Department of Engineering Science](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/) at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), working with Prof. [Antonis Papachristodoulou](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/antonis-papachristodoulou/) and Dr. [Konstantinos Gatsis](https://kgatsis.github.io/) in the Control Engineering Group.

My research sits at the intersection of control theory and machine learning: algorithms that learn efficiently, respect safety and stability constraints, and remain interpretable enough to be useful in real systems. Outside academia, I play violin with the Hertford College Orchestra.
