I am a DPhil candidate in the [Department of Engineering Science](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/) at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), as a member of the [Control Engineering Group](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/control/), supervised by Prof. [Antonis Papachristodoulou](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/antonis-papachristodoulou/) and Dr. [Konstantinos Gatsis](https://kgatsis.github.io/).

My research sits at the intersection of control theory and machine learning: learning-based control with guarantees, control-informed machine learning, safe and reliable generative models, and Neural ODEs.

Prior to Oxford, I completed an MSc in Control Systems at Imperial College London and a BEng in Detection, Guidance and Control at Northwestern Polytechnical University. Outside academia, I play violin with the Hertford College Orchestra.
